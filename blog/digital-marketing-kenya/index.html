Digital marketing for Kenyan businesses: where should you start?

The best starting point is not a platform. It is a clear customer, a relevant offer and a simple route from discovery to enquiry.

Quick takeaway: start with the customer's problem and the outcome you want. Tools and channels should support that goal, not distract from it.

1. Define the customer and decision

Be specific about who you want to reach, what they need, what makes the decision difficult and what would make them trust you. A property buyer, hotel guest, school parent and SaaS decision-maker need different proof and messages.

2. Make the offer easy to understand

Explain the result you help create, what is included and what a sensible first step looks like. Avoid listing every service before the customer understands the value.

3. Choose channels by customer behaviour

Use search and SEO when people are actively looking, social content to earn attention and demonstrate expertise, and paid ads to test a clear offer with a relevant audience. WhatsApp can make the enquiry step familiar and direct.

4. Build a conversion path before spending

A campaign should lead to a focused landing page or WhatsApp conversation. Ask only the information needed to qualify the enquiry, then respond promptly with a useful next step.

5. Measure meaningful outcomes

Track qualified enquiries, booked calls, sales opportunities and acquisition cost—not just impressions or likes. Review results and improve one bottleneck at a time.
