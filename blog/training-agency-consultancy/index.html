BUSINESS STRATEGY

Training, agency or consultancy: which kind of support do you need?

Choose support based on the gap you need to close: skills, execution or decision-making clarity.

Quick takeaway: start with the customer's problem and the outcome you want. Tools and channels should support that goal, not distract from it.

Choose training when capability is the gap

Training is a good fit when a person or team needs to learn how to use tools, follow a process or improve a skill. Examples include AI at work, digital marketing, sales enablement and leadership workshops.

Choose agency support when delivery is the gap

Agency services help when the work needs to be planned and executed: social media management, paid campaigns, SEO, content production or website development.

Choose consultancy when clarity is the gap

Consultancy helps you diagnose a challenge, prioritise options and make a plan. Examples include go-to-market strategy, digital transformation, marketing audits, customer journey design and AI adoption planning.

Combine them when the work calls for it

A company might begin with a marketing strategy, train its internal team, then use agency support for campaign execution. Or it may build a website and train staff to manage content after launch.

Start with the business outcome

Before choosing a package, describe what needs to change, by when, who is involved and how success will be measured. The best service path should follow that context.
