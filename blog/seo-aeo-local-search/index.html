SEO & AEO

SEO, answer engines and local search: the simple business checklist

The goal is to make your business easy to understand, useful to the searcher and consistent across the places people discover you.

Quick takeaway: start with the customer's problem and the outcome you want. Tools and channels should support that goal, not distract from it.

Answer real customer questions

Create pages that explain services, pricing factors, process, eligibility and common concerns. Direct answers help people and can make content easier for search systems to interpret.

Use descriptive page structure

Give each important service a unique title, a clear H1, useful subheadings and concise copy. Link related services and supporting articles together.

Keep business details consistent

Use the same business name, contact information and location details across your website and legitimate business profiles. Keep social profile links current.

Add structured data accurately

Organization, LocalBusiness or ProfessionalService, Person, BreadcrumbList and Article markup may help describe a page. Only include facts that are visible on the site and genuinely accurate.

Build reputation through useful work

Publish original guidance, show genuine experience, earn relevant mentions and collect authentic reviews where the platform allows them. Avoid keyword stuffing and manufactured reviews.
