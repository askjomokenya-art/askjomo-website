PAID ADS

Before running Meta Ads: make sure the offer and follow-up are ready

Paid reach can create attention, but the offer and customer journey determine whether that attention becomes a useful enquiry.

Quick takeaway: start with the customer's problem and the outcome you want. Tools and channels should support that goal, not distract from it.

Make the offer specific

State who it is for, the problem it solves, what the customer receives and what they should do next. A clear offer is easier to test than a general claim about quality.

Match the creative to the audience

Use a relevant hook, show the product or outcome clearly and keep the message aligned with the landing page. Do not promise results you cannot substantiate.

Choose the right destination

A landing page or WhatsApp conversation should continue the same message from the ad. Avoid sending every campaign to a generic homepage if a focused destination is more relevant.

Plan lead qualification

Ask a small number of useful questions—such as service needed, business type, location or timing—so the team can prioritise enquiries without unnecessary friction.

Measure the full journey

Review cost per qualified lead and downstream sales conversations alongside reach and clicks. Follow platform and privacy requirements, and test one meaningful change at a time.
