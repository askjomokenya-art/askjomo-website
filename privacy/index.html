PRIVACY

A simple privacy note.

This website is a static information and enquiry site.

What this website collects

The enquiry form runs in your browser and prepares a message to send through WhatsApp. The website itself does not send the form to a database or store your answers.

WhatsApp and external links

When you choose a WhatsApp link, you leave this website and use WhatsApp. WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy apply to information you send there. Social and professional profile links are operated by third parties.

Analytics and cookies

This starter version does not include analytics, advertising pixels or a cookie banner. If analytics or advertising tools are added later, update this notice and implement any required consent or privacy controls.

Contact

For questions, contact Ask Jomo Kenya through WhatsApp.
